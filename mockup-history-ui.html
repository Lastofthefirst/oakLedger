Profile-Specific History UI

Three approaches for surfacing that invoice history is tied to the active business profile.

Approach A

Subtitle in Card Header Implemented

The history card header includes a subtitle line showing the profile name and count: "Acme Corp — 12 invoices". When you switch profiles, the card updates automatically. The toolbar History button toggles the card open/closed.

Approach B

Tabs Inside the History Card

Profile tabs appear inside the history card itself, mirroring the business profile tabs pattern. You can flip between profiles' histories without changing the active business profile. Each tab shows the profile name and a count badge.

  • Could be confusing: "am I switching profiles?"
  • Export/Import/Clear need to be scoped carefully

Approach C

Color-Coded Profile Indicator

A colored left border on the history card matches the active profile's assigned color. The card header shows the profile name prominently. Each profile gets a distinct accent (e.g., gold, teal, indigo, coral) that appears on both the business tab and the history card, creating a visual link between the two.
